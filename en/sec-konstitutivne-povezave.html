Chapter 3 Constitutive equations

3.7 Connections

The subject abbreviations are the same as in section 1.19.

  • Hooke's law for an isotropic material: the axial, bending and shear stiffness of bars in bending (MK, VT, chapter 5), the torsional stiffness (MK, VD, chapter 16), the bending stiffness of plates (MK, chapter 18), the Navier-Lamé equations (VT, chapter 11), the material matrix of a finite element (AK, chapter 15).
  • Plane stress and plane strain, which follow from Hooke's law with some components set to zero: chapters 11 and 15 (VT, AK).
  • Strains caused by temperature: thermal loads on structures (VT, AK, chapters 14 and 15).
  • Anisotropic materials: composite plates and shells (MK, chapters 18 and 22).
  • The integral: work, energy and sums over a body in all later chapters, numerical integration in finite elements (AK, chapter 15).
  • The strain energy density and the symmetry of the elastic constants: energy methods (all subjects, chapter 4), the symmetry of the stiffness matrix (AK, chapter 7). Materials that do not return all the work on unloading, like rubber and polymers, are the basis of damping (DSK, chapter 20).
