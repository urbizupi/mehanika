Chapter 15 Continuum finite elements

15.11 Connections

The abbreviations of the courses are the same as in section 1.19.

  • The mesh and its quality, the discretization error and convergence (AK, chapter 17): in this chapter we compared \(h\) and the number of unknowns on the examples of the shell and the point force, general error estimators are there.
  • Symmetry and antisymmetry: the octant of the shell used three planes of reflection (MK, AK, chapter 19).
  • The mass matrix of continuum elements (DSK, chapters 21 and 10): for the bar we obtained it in lemma 10.31, the same idea holds for the brick and the tetrahedron.
  • Contact and the point force (AK, chapter 25).
  • Plates and shells (AK, chapters 18 and 22) use the same elements with a reduced number of degrees of freedom.
  • Heat conduction in a body (AK, VT, chapter 14): the conduction matrix of the tetrahedron from lemma 15.71 is used also by the spatial heat problem, with thermal stresses by lemma 15.73.
  • The wave equation in a body (DSK, chapter 13, theorem 13.24): for bodies with several dimensions it needs continuum elements.

Planned in other chapters: the generalized plane strain state (theorem 11.35) as an element, which the sources of the project do not treat (BOWER, PDF pp. 229-230), mixed elements and elements with pressure for incompressibility (COOK §13.6, PDF pp. 517-518), elements of higher order and the Lagrange Q9 (ONATE1 §5.3, PDF pp. 184-189), error estimation and stress recovery at optimal points (ONATE1 §6.7, PDF pp. 229-231), infinite elements and nonlinear material.
