This page holds short answers to the questions of the weekly tests. Each answer is what one would write in a test in about five minutes: the definitions and equations to be written down, with a short note on what they mean and where they stop holding, without derivations. Derivations, calculations and examples are in the sections of the book named in the line "In the book" under each answer. The concepts in the text are linked to their definitions, so a definition opens with one click. Each week we add a new section.
