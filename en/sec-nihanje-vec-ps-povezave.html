Chapter 10 Vibration of systems with several degrees of freedom and modal analysis (nihanje sistemov z več prostostnimi stopnjami in modalna analiza)

10.13 Connections (povezave)

The abbreviations of subjects are the same as in section 1.19.

  • Continuous systems: a string, a shaft and a beam have infinitely many degrees of freedom, their modes are functions, not columns (VD, DSK, chapter 13). The exact frequencies of the bar \(\omega_{\mathrm{r}} = (2r - 1)\pi c/(2L)\), with which we compared finite elements in section 10.7, are derived there, as are the upper bound of the Ritz method for continuous systems (section 10.8 and chapter 6) and the orthogonality of continuous modes, which is theorem 10.13 with an integral instead of a sum.
  • Beam and frame elements: the consistent mass matrix of the bar element from section 10.7 is repeated for the beam with the rotation of the node, where it is given by the kinetic energy of the displacement field of the beam, and so is the geometric stiffness, given by the axial forces in the cross sections, because of which the stiffness against transverse displacement changes (DSK, AK, chapter 12).
  • Damping models: complex modes, the state-space form, hysteretic and viscoelastic damping, which we only named in section 10.9 for the frame with one damper (DSK, chapter 20).
  • Eigenvalue algorithms, the truncated modal computation with the correction by the mode acceleration (popravek pospeška načina, mode-acceleration), the effective modal mass, the additional mass method, Guyan reduction and substructure assembly (DSK, chapter 21). The truncation of the modal sum to one mode, which for bar C gives \(81{,}1\) % of the static displacement of the end (section 10.9), is corrected there, and the numerical solution of large eigenproblems, which we only named in section 10.7, is explained there.
  • A machine on mounts that rocks (section 10.1) and balancing (DSK, chapter 23), rotors (DSK, chapter 24) and flexible robot links written with modes (DSK, chapter 28).
  • Time stepping: nonlinear systems and the choice of schemes with numerical damping (AK, chapter 17), where every step is solved by iteration.
