Chapter 5 Beam theory (teorija nosilcev)

5.16 Connections

The abbreviations of subjects are the same as in section 1.19.

  • The Euler-Bernoulli equation and boundary conditions: beam finite elements with Hermite interpolation functions (AK, MK, chapter 12), the virtual work equation for a beam and the computation with trial functions (chapter 6), plates as the two-dimensional version (MK, chapter 18).
  • Virtual work and natural boundary conditions: the virtual work equation in integral form (chapter 6), Hamilton's principle and dynamic boundary conditions (VD, DSK, chapters 8 and 13).
  • The Timoshenko beam: shear locking (AK, chapter 12), Mindlin plates (chapter 18), rotary inertia in vibration (DSK, chapter 13).
  • Moments of inertia and principal axes: torsion (MK, VD, chapter 16), rotor shafts (DSK, chapter 24).
  • Deflection and compliance: compliant links of robots (DSK, chapter 28), influence coefficients (chapter 10).
  • Frames: spatial frames and assembly (AK, MK, chapter 12), symmetry of frames (MK, chapter 19).
  • Limits of the theory: plane solutions of elasticity for beams with the Airy function (VT, MK, chapter 11), the hierarchy of models (AK, chapter 17).
