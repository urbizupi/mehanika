Chapter 7 Basics of FEM for bars and trusses

7.11 Connections (povezave)

The abbreviations of the courses are the same as in section 1.19.

  • Element matrix, assembly and supports: beam and frame elements with the rotation of a node (MK, AK, chapter 12, route (b) from section 7.5.1), elements for a continuum and the test with a small sample (patch test) (AK, chapter 15, ONATE1 §3.9, PDF p. 126), elements for heat conduction (AK, VT, chapter 14), where assembly is repeated with temperatures as the unknowns.
  • The mass matrix of a bar element and vibration of trusses (DSK, chapters 10 and 13).
  • Banded and sparse solution of large models (AK, chapter 17), modal reduction (DSK, chapter 21).
  • Symmetry and antisymmetry of structures (MK, AK, chapter 19).
  • Trusses and optimization of the cross-sections of their bars (MK, chapter 29).
  • Compliant links of robots (DSK, chapter 28): an element with free nodes has a rigid motion in which nothing deforms and there is no force, like the unsupported bar in section 7.3 (SHABANA, PDF pp. 280-281). SHABANA removes the rigid motion of an element with reference conditions that fix relative to what the displacement is measured (SHABANA §6.2, PDF p. 286). This is similar to the supports in section 7.3, but not the same: a support pins the body, while a reference condition only decides what we count as deformation, and the rigid motion is described by other coordinates. The link with compliant mechanisms as structures with intended mechanisms is not supported by the sources of the chapter, so we do not state it.
