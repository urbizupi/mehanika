Chapter 8 Analytical dynamics (analitična dinamika)

8.15 Connections (povezave)

The abbreviations of the courses are the same as in section 1.19.

  • Lagrange's equations and small oscillations: one degree of freedom with damping and excitation (VD 5-6, DSK, chapter 9), also impulse loading (GR §1.5), \(n\) degrees of freedom and modes of oscillation (VD 7-10, chapter 10), damping models (chapter 20), modal reduction (chapter 21).
  • Hamilton's principle for a continuum: strings, shafts and beams (VD 11-12, DSK 2, chapter 13), plates (chapter 18).
  • A rigid body in space and gyroscopic forces (chapter 24), crank mechanisms (chapter 23), manipulators with Lagrange's equations (DSK 13, chapter 27, SPONG).
  • Constraints and multipliers in the finite element method, the penalty method and multipliers (chapters 12 and 19), geometric stiffness and linear buckling analysis (chapter 12, COOK, chapter 18).
  • Stability of plates: buckling of a circular plate (EP §7.2.2.2, p. 411 to 414) and of a rectangular plate (EP §7.2.4.3, p. 445 to 447, and §7.2.8, p. 476 to 485), chapter 18. Stability of shells the sources of the project do not treat.
