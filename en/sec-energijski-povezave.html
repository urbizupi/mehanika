Chapter 4 Energy principles and virtual work

4.9 Connections

The subject abbreviations are the same as in section 1.19.

  • The principle of virtual displacements: the virtual work equation for problems with boundary conditions and the computation with trial fields (AK, MK, DSK, chapter 6), the computation of bars piece by piece (AK, MK, chapter 7), virtual work for the bending and axial loading of a bar (VT 8, MK, chapter 5), virtual work for mechanisms in generalized coordinates and d'Alembert's principle (VD 1, DSK 2, chapter 8).
  • The principle of minimum total potential energy: the stiffness matrix from the second derivatives of the energy (AK, chapter 7), Dirichlet's condition for the stability of an equilibrium (VD, chapter 8), Rayleigh's method for natural frequencies (DSK, chapter 13), structural optimization with minimum compliance (MK, chapter 29).
  • Virtual forces, the unit dummy load method and Castigliano's theorems: displacements and statically indeterminate structures made of bent bars joined rigidly to each other (MK, VT, chapter 5).
  • Reciprocity and the influence lines of displacements, as Mohr drew them (TIMO, PDF p. 224, MK, VT, chapter 5).
  • Betti's and Maxwell's theorems: the symmetry of the stiffness and flexibility matrices (AK, chapter 7), influence coefficients (VD, DSK, chapter 10).
  • Integrals over a body and the divergence theorem: the equilibrium of a finite part of a body and the problem with prescribed conditions on the boundary (VT, chapter 11), the virtual work equation in integral form (chapter 6), heat conduction (AK, VT, chapter 14).
